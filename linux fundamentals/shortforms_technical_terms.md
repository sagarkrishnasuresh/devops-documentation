PCIe: Peripheral Component Interconnect Express  is a high-speed serial interface standard used to connect expansion cards and internal components—such as graphics cards, NVMe SSDs, network cards, and sound cards—to a computer's motherboard.
• NVMe is the protocol (how the drive and system talk).
• PCIe is the bus/interface (the lanes carrying the data).
• M.2 is a common physical shape/slot
